Capacity-Constrained Online Bin-Packing Formulation

Mathematical specification & partitioning mechanics of the 5-Minute / 5-Item Batching Engine

Mathematical Formulation

Time Window Constraint:

\( \forall o_i \in \text{Batch}_k, \quad |t(o_i) - t_0(\text{Batch}_k)| \le \Delta T \quad (\Delta T = 300\text{s}) \)

Item Capacity Limit:

\( \sum_{o_i \in \text{Batch}_k} \text{Items}(o_i) \le C_{\text{max}} \quad (C_{\text{max}} = 5\text{ items}) \)

Weight Capacity Limit:

\( \sum_{o_i \in \text{Batch}_k} \text{Weight}(o_i) \le W_{\text{max}} \quad (W_{\text{max}} = 4.5\text{ kg}) \)

Splitting Invariant

When an incoming order \( O_{\text{new}} \) with \( m \) items arrives for Zone \( Z \):

  • If \( |\text{Batch}_Z| + m \le 5 \): Append directly to current batch.
  • If \( |\text{Batch}_Z| + m > 5 \): Greedily fill current batch to exactly 5 items (\(\text{Batch}_Z\)-A), and automatically instantiate an overflow batch (\(\text{Batch}_Z\)-B) for the same zone within the same active time window.
  • If a single massive order has \( m > 10 \): Recursively partitions across \( \lceil m / 5 \rceil \) batches.
